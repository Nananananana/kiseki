# ADR-0049: Mixed evidence is stated, never resolved



## Status

Accepted. Delivers proposals/0004, v0.6 item 2.

## Context

A summary wants one story: "you moved from nature to the city". The
evidence often holds two: nature stayed strong, and city visits
grew. proposals/0004 forbids resolving that into a contradiction or
flattening one person into one category, and proposals/0006 keeps
the rule for the heavier cross-timeline work coming in v0.8.

## Decision

Every enduring finding is paired with every rising one, the loudest
products first, capped at three. The pairs are stated -- "museum
stayed strong while ramen grew" -- with the numbers beside them, in
`kiseki insights` and its payload ("mixed"). Nothing is resolved,
ranked as conflict, or judged; a pair is two true things side by
side. Deterministic, derived on demand, stored nowhere, no model.

## Consequences

- A reader is never shown a rise without what quietly held.
- v0.8's cross-timeline analysis inherits the posture: co-occurrence
  is reported as co-occurrence, and never a causal claim.
- Next per proposals/0004: personal place intelligence.
