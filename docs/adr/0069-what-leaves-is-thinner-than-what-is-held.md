# ADR-0069: What leaves is thinner than what is held

## Status

Accepted. Extends ADR-0047 after reading a real export.

## Context

The interest export refused places, identifiers, coordinates and
exact times, and let everything else out. On a real library that was
six hundred and ninety-five topics, and the tail of the list was not
the owner: pharmaceutical software read off an office screen, a word
seen once in a photograph of a document, a certificate they were
studying for. Every one of those is a true reading. None of them is
something to hand to anybody.

The obvious answer -- a list of sensitive words -- was refused. Such a
list encodes one person's idea of what is private, is wrong for the
next person, and grows by taste rather than by rule. The label
stoplist earns its place because "a word about the record rather than
the world" is a test anyone can apply (ADR-0053); "a word about
something private" is not.

## Decision

The gate is evidence. A topic leaves when at least three separate
readings support it and the library's own confidence in it reaches
0.3. Places remain forbidden outright, as before.

This is not a claim about what is sensitive. It is a claim about what
has been shown: twice is a pair of occasions, three times is a habit
of the evidence, and a topic the library half-believes is not
something to send anywhere. The work screen that appears in fifty
photographs still leaves -- and it should, because by then it is
genuinely part of the owner's days.

Stages leave only for topics the interests carried out with them, so
the two halves of the document can never disagree.

## Consequences

- Roughly five hundred readings stay home, and what leaves is what
  repeated.
- Nothing changes about what is stored or what the owner sees. The
  library is as candid as ever with the person it belongs to.
- Phase 3, the anonymous interest community, receives a stronger
  document than it would have: an interest that appeared once was
  never going to mean anything across people either.
