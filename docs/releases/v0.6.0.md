# KISEKI v0.6.0



Understand change, and let it point forward. v0.6 turns the derived
context into a feed worth reading and suggestions worth taking:
discovery ranked by novelty and importance, coexisting tendencies
held side by side, your own places read back to you, questions
scoped to a place, retrieval that names its channels and can no
longer be starved, and the first suggestions -- all from your own
evidence, deterministic, local, nothing new stored about you.

## Highlights

- **Discovery ranks by novelty and importance** (ADR-0048).
  `kiseki discover` / GET /discover rank the insights by novelty
  times importance -- magnitude scaled by remaining evidence.
  Confidence is shown and never ranked on; similarity never enters;
  no read-state exists.
- **Mixed evidence is stated, never resolved** (ADR-0049). An
  enduring interest and a rising one are held together -- "museum
  stayed strong while ramen grew" -- in `kiseki insights` and its
  payload, never flattened into a category or a contradiction.
- **Places the evidence knows.** `kiseki places` clusters your
  journeys' stops deterministically and states, per place: visits,
  first and last, and the median revisit gap.
- **Ask keeps evidence within reach.** `kiseki ask --near "lat,lon"`
  (--within-km) scopes a question to a place the way a time window
  scopes it to a year. The index stays coordinate-free (ADR-0036):
  locations are read from the primary store at question time, and a
  screen reading never matches a place condition.
- **Retrieval names its channels and filters before ranks**
  (proposals/0006). Every retrieval carries the channels that found
  it (words, meaning); structured filters apply inside each channel,
  and a filtered question asks the channels deeper, so a place
  question cannot be starved by the rest of the corpus. A model-free
  golden retrieval dataset (tests/golden/retrieval.json) runs in CI
  and caught the starvation twice before it shipped.
- **Suggestions come from your own evidence** (ADR-0050).
  `kiseki suggest` offers go back (a place with a revisit cadence,
  overdue past twice that cadence) and pick up (an interest dormant
  after several readings) -- the why always arithmetic, references
  in the profile vocabulary so `kiseki correct` can decline a
  suggestion.

## Moved

- Prompt-version tracking (proposals/0004, v0.6 item 5) lands at the
