# KISEKI v0.4.0



Ask your own library. v0.4 turns the readings KISEKI already makes
into things you can question: search over your own captions, answers
with evidence and a derived confidence, places that speak by name,
and a lifecycle for every topic -- all local, nothing new stored
about you.

## Highlights

- **Single photographs join the profile** (FR-507, ADR-0033 to
  ADR-0035). Roughly a third of an ordinary library forms no stop at
  all -- a dish, a shop window, a cat. Each now gets its own caption,
  feeds the subjects and the profile, and consent governs the stay
  captions' representative selection end to end.
- **A search index beside the stores** (ADR-0036). Answered stay
  captions, single captions and labelled screen readings become FTS5
  documents plus per-model vectors in the same SQLite database.
  Resumable `kiseki index`; withheld photographs and sensitive
  screens are never indexed, and no coordinate enters a document.
- **`kiseki ask` and GET /ask** (ADR-0037, ADR-0038). Deterministic
  hybrid retrieval (words and meaning, fused by reciprocal rank)
  chooses numbered facts; the model phrases one cited answer over
  that closed list. Confidence, time range and evidence derive from
  the retrieval, never from the model; with no evidence there is no
  model call.
- **Time in the question** (ADR-0039). "Last year", kyonen,
  YYYY-nen M-gatsu, koko-N-days and friends become the window
  deterministically; explicit --since/--until override the words;
  the applied window travels in the answer contract.
- **Named places** (ADR-0040). An offline GeoNames file you download
  yourself (docs/gazetteer.md) names place topics at presentation
  time in `profile` and `view`. Anchors are never named; names are
  never stored.
- **Places speak in the story** (ADR-0041). `kiseki tell` narrates
  the named places and quotes the single captions photographed
  within 500 m of them; /tell over HTTP stays place-silent.
- **Lifecycle labels** (ADR-0042). New, returned, growing,
  declining, dormant, stable -- derived from the whole kept profile
  history and stored nowhere; `kiseki lifecycle` and GET /lifecycle.

## Upgrading

- No schema change: v0.3 databases work as they are (version 4).
- New commands: `singles`, `index`, `ask`, `lifecycle`.
- Run `kiseki singles` once, then `kiseki index`, to make the new
  answers available; add both to your refresh routine
  (docs/runbook.md).
- Optional: download the GeoNames cities500 file for place names
  (docs/gazetteer.md). Without it, everything works as before.
