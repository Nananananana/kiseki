# KISEKI v0.5.0



Discover yourself, correct the record. v0.5 turns the readings into
findings you can inspect, question and overrule: deterministic
insights with evidence, corrections that reach every derivation,
comparisons that show their arithmetic, a privacy dashboard counted
from your own data, and a versioned one-way export -- all local,
nothing new stored about you, and the model still never invents a
fact.

## Highlights

- **Insights are derived, never invented** (ADR-0043). New,
  returned, rising, declining, dormant, enduring -- classified from
  the lifecycle and trend machinery, with novelty as a fixed
  per-kind constant, confidence and evidence reused from the
  profile, and derived_from naming the sources. `kiseki insights`
  (--story narrates over a closed, cited fact list), GET /insights,
  and `ask` attaches matched findings as supporting_insights --
  metadata the model never sees.
- **Corrections are appended, applied at read** (ADR-0044). An
  append-only log in the evidence vocabulary (topic:, caption:,
  photo:, screen:), latest word wins, undo is another append. A pure
  read-time filter reaches profile, trend, lifecycle, insights,
  tell, view, supporting_insights and ask retrieval; raw evidence
  and kept profiles are never rewritten. `kiseki correct` /
  `kiseki corrections`.
- **Comparisons carry their arithmetic** (ADR-0045). Appeared, gone,
  stronger, weaker, steady -- movement past the trend's own delta --
  with strengths and evidence counts on both sides. `kiseki compare`
  (--from/--to) and GET /compare; the lifecycle rows now show the
  same arithmetic ("now x, was y").
- **Privacy is a report, not a promise** (ADR-0046).
  `kiseki privacy` counts, from storage, what is stored, what the
  owner has withheld, and what is never stored by construction. Its
  first run exposed a real wiring gap (screens missing from two
  pipeline builders), now fixed.
- **Export is a one-way abstraction** (ADR-0047). `kiseki export`
  writes kiseki-interest-export v1: interests with month-level time
  plus lifecycle stages. Never a place topic, an identifier, an
  exact timestamp or a coordinate.
- **The doctor speaks in categories**. `kiseki doctor` runs
  deterministic checks -- [schema], [integrity], [privacy],
  [evidence] (the snapshot opportunity hint), [consistency] -- and
  fixes nothing.
- Console-safe names: the gazetteer prefers GeoNames asciiname, and
  the CLI degrades instead of crashing on a narrow console encoding.

## Upgrading

- Additive schema only: the corrections table appears on connect;
  v0.4 databases work as they are (version 4).
- New commands: `insights`, `correct`, `corrections`, `compare`,
  `privacy`, `export`, `doctor`.
- The weekly `kiseki profile` habit now feeds insights and compare
  as well; `kiseki doctor` tells you when a snapshot is worth
  taking.
